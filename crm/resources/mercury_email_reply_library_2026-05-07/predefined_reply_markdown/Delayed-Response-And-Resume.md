## Use Case

Use when a client waited for a reply and we need to acknowledge the delay while moving the matter forward.

## Client-Facing Reply

```text
Hello [Client Name],

Thank you for your patience. I apologize for the delay in getting back to you.

I am reviewing your message now and will make sure it is routed to the correct department for the next step. To keep the request properly tracked, please continue this matter through the secure client portal when possible:

https://uscapitalprivatebank.com/crm/

If there is a specific document, question, or deadline you want us to prioritize, please reply with that detail and we will attach it to the request.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Do Not Use When

Do not use if the message involves wires, deposits, balances, legal threats, suspicious links, credentials, account access, or regulatory/compliance issues. Escalate those to Aurora/Morpheus/Locke.
