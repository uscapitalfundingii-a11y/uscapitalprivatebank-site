# USCPB Safe - Broker Or Intermediary Routing

This file follows the root workspace instructions in `D:\GithubRepos\AGENTS.md`.

Owner: Aurora  
Primary user: Mercury  
Channel: Email, CRM ticket, chat  
Risk level: Medium

## Use Case

Use when a broker, intermediary, introducer, mandate, advisor, or third-party representative contacts us about a client or transaction.

## Client-Facing Reply

```text
Hello [Client Name],

Thank you for your message. If you are acting on behalf of a client or principal, please provide documentation showing your authority to communicate for that party.

For proper routing, please submit the request and supporting authorization through the secure client portal:

https://uscapitalprivatebank.com/crm/

Once the authorization and request details are received, our team can determine the appropriate department for review.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Do Not Use When

Do not confirm deal acceptance, client status, account details, transaction status, payment instructions, or instrument validity to a third party without approved authorization.
