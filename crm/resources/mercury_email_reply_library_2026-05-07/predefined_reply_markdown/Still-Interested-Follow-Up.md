# USCPB Safe - Still Interested Follow Up

Owner: Aurora  
Primary user: Mercury  
Channel: Email, CRM ticket, chat  
Risk level: Low

## Use Case

Use for a gentle follow-up when a client has not responded and there is no open sensitive issue.

## Client-Facing Reply

```text
Hello [Client Name],

I wanted to follow up and see whether you would still like assistance with your U.S. Capital Private Bank request.

If you would like to continue, please reply with the best next step or submit/update your request through the secure client portal:

https://uscapitalprivatebank.com/crm/

Our team can then route the matter to the correct department and help keep the process moving.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Do Not Use When

Do not use for suppressed contacts, opt-outs, compliance disputes, bounced invalid addresses, or anyone who asked not to be contacted.
