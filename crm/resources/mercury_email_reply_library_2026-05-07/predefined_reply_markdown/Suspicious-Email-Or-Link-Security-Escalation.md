# USCPB Safe - Suspicious Email Or Link Security Escalation

This file follows the root workspace instructions in `D:\GithubRepos\AGENTS.md`.

Owner: Aurora  
Primary user: Mercury  
Channel: Email, CRM ticket, chat  
Risk level: High

## Use Case

Use when a client reports a suspicious email, link, attachment, payment change, login alert, or possible impersonation.

## Client-Facing Reply

```text
Hello [Client Name],

Thank you for reporting this. Please do not click any suspicious links, open unexpected attachments, or share passwords, codes, recovery phrases, payment details, or account credentials.

Our security team should review the message before any further action is taken. Please forward only the suspicious message details or screenshot through the secure support portal if possible:

https://uscapitalprivatebank.com/crm/

If you believe an account or device may already be compromised, please tell us immediately and change your password from a trusted device.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Do Not Use When

Do not ask the client to send passwords, one-time codes, private keys, seed phrases, or full account credentials. Escalate to Locke.
