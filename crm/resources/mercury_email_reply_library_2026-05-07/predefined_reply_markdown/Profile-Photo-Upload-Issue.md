## Use Case

Use when a client or staff member cannot find the photo upload/browse button or needs help changing a profile photo.

## Client-Facing Reply

```text
Hello [Client Name],

Thank you for letting us know. We are checking the profile photo upload option so clients and staff can update their photo properly.

Please log in to the secure portal, open your profile page, and check whether the upload or browse option appears near the profile image area:

https://uscapitalprivatebank.com/crm/

If the button still does not appear, please reply with your device type, browser, and a screenshot of the profile section so our CRM team can troubleshoot it.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Do Not Use When

Do not request passwords, codes, private IDs, or sensitive screenshots.
