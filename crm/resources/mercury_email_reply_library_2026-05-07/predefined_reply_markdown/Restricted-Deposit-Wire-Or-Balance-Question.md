# USCPB Safe - Restricted Deposit Wire Or Balance Question

This file follows the root workspace instructions in `D:\GithubRepos\AGENTS.md`.

Owner: Aurora  
Primary user: Mercury  
Channel: Email, CRM ticket, chat  
Risk level: High

## Use Case

Use when someone asks about deposits, wires, balances, ledger entries, account funds, transaction posting, or money movement.

## Client-Facing Reply

```text
Hello [Client Name],

Thank you for your message. For security reasons, deposit, wire, balance, and account-transaction matters must be handled through the secure client portal and reviewed by the appropriate department.

Please submit the request through the portal and include any non-sensitive reference details that help identify the matter:

https://uscapitalprivatebank.com/crm/

We cannot provide wire instructions, confirm balances, approve deposits, or verify transaction outcomes through ordinary email.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Do Not Use When

Do not include account numbers, wire instructions, balance confirmations, payment directions, wallet details, or private client records in email.
