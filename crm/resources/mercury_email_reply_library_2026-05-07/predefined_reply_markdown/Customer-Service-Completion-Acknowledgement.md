Owner: Aurora

Channel: Email, CRM ticket, chat  
Risk level: Low

## Use Case

Use when acknowledging that a support item was received, completed, updated, or routed.

## Client-Facing Reply

```text
Hello [Client Name],

Thank you for the update. We have recorded your request and routed it to the appropriate support area.

If there is anything else you would like added to the same matter, please reply in the same thread or update the request through the secure client portal:

https://uscapitalprivatebank.com/crm/

Keeping the information in one place helps our team respond faster and avoid duplicate handling.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Do Not Use When

Do not use to imply that a financial, legal, compliance, transaction, KYC, or account decision has been approved.
