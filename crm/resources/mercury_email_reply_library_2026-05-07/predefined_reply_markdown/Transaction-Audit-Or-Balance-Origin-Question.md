Channel: Email, CRM ticket, admin-facing notes  
Risk level: High

## Use Case

Use when a client or staff member asks where a balance, ledger transfer, deposit, account credit, or transaction originated.

## Client-Facing Reply

```text
Hello [Client Name],

Thank you for your message. Account balance, ledger-transfer, deposit-origin, and transaction-audit questions must be reviewed through the secure support workflow.

Please submit the request through the client portal and include any reference number, date, account name, or transaction description available to you:

https://uscapitalprivatebank.com/crm/

For security and audit reasons, we cannot confirm sensitive account or transaction details through ordinary email until the request is reviewed through the proper channel.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Internal Note

Morpheus should preserve audit data showing who initiated the entry, when it occurred, which account was affected, and whether the initiator had authorization. Do not remove funds or records without administrator review and audit logging.
