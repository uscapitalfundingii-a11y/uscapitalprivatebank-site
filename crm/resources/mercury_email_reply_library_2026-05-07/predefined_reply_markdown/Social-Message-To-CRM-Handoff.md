This file follows the root workspace instructions in `D:\GithubRepos\AGENTS.md`.

Owner: Aurora  
Primary user: Mercury, Hermes  
Channel: WhatsApp, Telegram, Teams, WeChat, Messenger, Instagram, email  
Risk level: Medium

## Use Case

Use when a client contacts through social messaging and the issue needs formal CRM tracking.

## Client-Facing Reply

```text
Hello [Client Name],

Thank you for reaching out. We can assist, but to keep this properly tracked and routed to the correct department, please submit or confirm the request through the secure client portal:

https://uscapitalprivatebank.com/crm/

You may continue to message us here for general assistance, but documents, account-service requests, transaction questions, and formal support items should be handled through the portal.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Do Not Use When

Do not collect passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, private keys, wire details, wallet information, or sensitive account records through social chat.
