## Use Case

Use when the client needs help uploading KYC, business, identity, or supporting documents.

## Client-Facing Reply

```text
Hello [Client Name],

Thank you for your message. For security and proper tracking, please upload KYC and supporting documents through the secure client portal rather than ordinary email:

https://uscapitalprivatebank.com/crm/

After logging in, open or update your support request and attach the documents there. Please make sure the files are clear, complete, and match the name or organization connected to the request.

Once uploaded, our team can review the submission and route it to the appropriate department.

Thank you,
[Signature]
```

## Do Not Use When

Do not state that KYC is approved, accepted, complete, or sufficient unless the responsible reviewer has confirmed it.
