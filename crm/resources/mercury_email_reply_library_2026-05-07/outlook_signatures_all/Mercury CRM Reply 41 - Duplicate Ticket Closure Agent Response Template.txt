🚫 Avoiding Duplicate Support Tickets

At US Capital Private Bank, our support portal is designed to ensure your inquiries and service requests are handled efficiently and effectively. To maintain the highest quality of service, we kindly ask all clients not to create duplicate tickets for the same issue or request.

📌 Why Duplicate Tickets Are Closed

-
Efficiency in Response: Creating multiple tickets for the same matter can slow down our ability to respond, as it splits resources and creates confusion in tracking.

-
Accurate Resolution: When all information is contained in a single ticket, our support team can review the full history and context, ensuring a more precise and timely resolution.

-
Clear Communication: A single ticket keeps all updates, files, and responses in one place, preventing miscommunication or overlooked details.
