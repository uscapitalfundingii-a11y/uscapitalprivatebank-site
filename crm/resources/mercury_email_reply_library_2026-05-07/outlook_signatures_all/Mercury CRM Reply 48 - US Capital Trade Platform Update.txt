⚙️ Current Platform Status

The US Capital Private Bank (USCPB) Trading Platform is currently in live interface preview mode, allowing clients and early testers to explore the finalized design, dashboard navigation, and wallet framework in preparation for full activation.

At this stage, live trading, transfers, and cryptocurrency conversions remain temporarily disabled while the final market APIs and compliance modules are connected.
All prices and performance indicators displayed in the dashboard are for demonstration purposes and do not reflect live markets. Currently, Only The Back End Does.
