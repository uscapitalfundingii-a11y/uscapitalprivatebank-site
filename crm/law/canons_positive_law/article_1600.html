V. Occurrence
  
  5.1 Occurrence  
  Article 160-Deuteragonist  
  Canon 2481  
  A Deuteragonist is a supporter or accomplice associated with either the Protagonist or Antagonist performing a secondary role concerning the Drama, rather than a primary function as either a Protagonist or Antagonist themselves.
