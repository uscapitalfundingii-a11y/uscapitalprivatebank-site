Trust Registration is the registration of a valid Ucadian Trust into a Ucadian Register or an alternate society register.  
     
  Trust Number Registration  
  The unique eighteen (18) digit trust number for your True Trust as well as your Divine Trust are valid registration numbers from the Great Register and Public Record of One Heaven - the highest possible register of all registers.  
  In turn, Ucadian societies are granted the right to manage the temporal recording of the Great Register and Public Record of One Heaven for their region on Earth.  
  Your Trust Number of obtained by following the instructions under "Obtaining and Understanding your Trust Numbers" here.  
     
  Registering your Trust on the Great Register  
  Once you have obtained your Trust Number, this does not mean you are registered on the temporal version of the Great Register of One Heaven. Instead, you need to register your trust number and details here: http://one-heaven.org/register_create_member.asp  
  (1) The first thing you will be asked to do is search for your Trust Number to make sure you are not creating a Duplicate record. If there are no existing records, a long button will appear saying "Trust Number Not Registered. Click here to Continue". Click the button to go to the next screen  
  (2) The next screen you will see is the data entry screen for inputting key information in order to complete the registration process. All the information requested is explained by clicking on the help icons. # NOTE: It is assumed that each living member has their own unique email address. If you try and register two different people with the same email address you will not be able to proceed.  
  (3) The next screen will ask you to confirm your details as well as identify your location at a community level.  
  (4) The final screen is your sacred oath as competent trustee. If you agree to this sacred oath and click "I Pledge my oath and agreement" then you have completed the registration process.
