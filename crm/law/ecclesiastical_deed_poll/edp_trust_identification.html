  Live Borne Record  
  A Live Borne Record is permitted for the purpose of identifying the valid birth of a Divine Trust and True Trust in the conduct of trade and commerce when proof of registration and birth is required to obtain certain services from an alternate society.  
  As many key services of alternate societies require the existence of some trust entity in which to engage, the Live Borne Record is proof of a True Trust, separate to any Roman Cestui Que Vie or other statutory/temporary trusts.
