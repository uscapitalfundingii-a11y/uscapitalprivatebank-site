Chapter - 35

1That which was written by Enoch. He wrote all this instruction of wisdom for every man of dignity, and every judge of the earth; for all my children who shall dwell upon earth, and for subsequent generations, conducting themselves uprightly and peaceably.
2Let not your spirit be grieved on account of the times; for the holy, the Great One, has prescribed a period to all.
3Let the righteous man arise from slumber; let him arise, and proceed in the path of righteousness, in all its paths; and let him advance in goodness and eternal clemency.
4Mercy shall be showed to the righteous man; upon him shall be conferred integrity and power for ever.
5In goodness and in righteousness shall he exist, and shall walk in everlasting light; but sin shall perish in eternal darkness, nor be seen from that time forward for evermore.
6After this, Enoch began to speak from a book.
7And Enoch said, Concerning the children of righteousness, concerning the elect of the world, and concerning the plant of righteousness and integrity.
8Concerning these things will I speak, and these things will I explain to you, my children: I who am Enoch.
9In consequence of that which has been shown to me, from my heavenly vision and from the voice of the Watchers and Holy Ones have I acquired knowledge; and from the tablet of heaven have I acquired understanding.
10Enoch then began to speak from a book, and said, I have been born the seventh in the first age, while judgment and righteousness wait with patience.
11But after me, in the second age, great wickedness shall arise, and fraud shall spring forth.
12In that age the end of the first shall take place, in which mankind shall be safe.
13But when the first is completed, iniquity shall grow up; and during the second age he shall execute the decree (of the deluge) upon sinners.
14Afterwards, in the third age, during its completion, a man of the plant of righteous judgment shall be selected; and after him the Plant of righteousness shall come for ever.
15Subsequently, in the fourth age, during its completion, the visions of the holy and the righteous shall be seen, the order of generation after generation shall take place, and a habitation shall be made for them.
16Then in the fifth age, during its completion, the house of glory and of dominion shall be erected for ever.
17After that, in the sixth age, all those who are in it shall be darkened, the hearts of all of them shall be forgetful of wisdom, and in it shall a Man arise and come forth.
18And during its completion He shall burn the house of dominion with fire, and all the race of the elect root shall be dispersed.
19Afterwards, in the seventh age, a perverse generation shall arise; abundant shall be its deeds, and all its deeds perverse.
20During its completion, the righteous shall be selected from the everlasting plant of righteousness; and to them shall be given the sevenfold doctrine of his whole creation.
21Afterwards there shall be another age, the eighth of righteousness, to which shall be given a sword to execute judgment and justice upon all oppressors.
22Sinners shall be delivered up into the hands of the righteous, who during its completion shall acquire habitations by their righteousness; and the house of the great King shall be established for celebrations for ever.
23After this, in the ninth age, shall the judgment of righteousness be revealed to the whole world.
24Every work of the ungodly shall disappear from the whole earth; the world shall be marked for destruction; and all men shall be on the watch for the path of integrity.
25And after this, on the seventh day of the tenth age, there shall be an everlasting judgment, which shall be executed upon the Watchers; and a spacious eternal heaven shall spring forth in the midst of the angels.
26The former heaven shall depart and pass away; a new heaven shall appear; and all the celestial powers shall shine with sevenfold splendour for ever.
27Afterwards likewise shall there be many weeks, which shall externally exist in goodness and in righteousness.
28Neither shall sin be named there for ever and for ever.
29Who is there of all the children of men, capable of hearing the voice of the Holy One without emotion?
30Who is there capable of thinking his thoughts? Who capable of contemplating all the workmanship of heaven? Who of comprehending the deeds of heaven?
31He may behold its animation, but not its spirit.
32He may be capable of conversing respecting it, but not of ascending to it.
33He may see all the boundaries of these things, and meditate upon them; but he can make nothing like them.
34Who of all men is able to understand the breadth and length of the earth?
35By whom have been seen the dimensions of all these things? Is it every man who is capable of comprehending the extent of heaven; what its elevation is, and by what it is supported?
36How many are the numbers of the stars; and where all the luminaries remain at rest?
