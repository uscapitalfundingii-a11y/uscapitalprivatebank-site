Chapter - 5

1After Aliyan Baal came, And came the Virgin Anath,
2They besought Lady Asherah of the Sea.
3Yea entreated the Creatress of the Gods.
4And Lady Asherah of the Sea replied: "How can Ye beseech Lady Asherah of the Sea,
5Yea entreat the Creatress of the Gods?
6Have Ye besought The Bull, God of Mercy,
7Or entreated the Creator of Creatures?
8And the Virgin Anath replied: "We do beseech Lady Asherah of the Sea.
9We entreat the Creatress of Gods.
10The Gods eat and drink,
11And those that suck the breast quaff With a keen knife A slice of fatling.
12They drink wine from a goblet,
13From a cup of gold, the blood of vines."
14Asherah of the Sea declares: "Saddle an ass,
15Hitch a donkey!
16Put on a harness of silver, Trappings of gold.
17Prepare the harness of My jennies!
18Qadish-u-Amrar hearkens.
19He saddles an ass Hitches a donkey.
20Put on a harness of silver, Trappings of gold.
21Prepares the harness of Her jennies!
22Qadish-u-Amrar embraces;
23He sets Asherah on the back of the ass,
24On the beautiful back of the donkey.
25Qadish begins to light the way,
26Even Amrar like a star.
27Forward goes the Virgin Anath,
28And Baal departs for the heights of Saphon.
29Then She sets face toward El,
30At the sources of the Two Rivers,
31In the midst of the streams of the Two Deeps.
32She enters the abode of El,
33And comes into the domicile of the King, Father Shunem.
34At the feet of El She bows and falls,
35She prostrates Herself and honors Him.
36As soon as El sees Her,
37He cracks a smile and laughs.
38His feet He sets on the footstool,
39And twiddles His fingers.
40He lifts His voice And shouts:"Why has Lady Asherah of the Sea come?
41Why came the Creatress of Gods?
42Art Thou hungry?
43Then have a morsel!
44Or art Thou thirsty?
45Then have a drink!
46Eat!
47Or drink!
48Eat bread from the tables!
49Drink wine from the goblets!
50From a cup of gold, the blood of vines!
51If the love of El moves Thee,
52Yea the affection of The Bull arouses Thee!"
53And Lady Asherah of the Sea replies: "Thy word, El, is wise;
54Thou art wise unto eternity;
55Lucky life is Thy word.
56Our king is Aliyan Baal,
57Out judge, and none is above Him.
58Let both of Us drain His chalice;
59Both of Us drain His cup!"
