Chapter - 6

1Loudly Bull-El, Her father, shouts,
2King El who brought Her into being;
3There shout Asherah and Her sons,
4The Goddess and the band of Her brood:
5"Lo there is no house unto Baal like the Gods.
6Not a court like the sons of Asherah:
7The dwelling of El,
8The shelter of His sons.
9The dwelling of Lady Asherah of the Sea,
10The dwelling of the renowned brides.
11The dwelling of Pidray, girl of Light.
12The shelter of Tallay, girl of rain.
13The dwelling of Arsay, girl of Yaabdar."
14And the God of Mercy replied: "Am I to act as a lackey of Asherah?
15Am I to act like the holder of a trowel?
16If the handmaid of Asherah will make the bricks
17A house shall be built for Baal like the Gods.
18Yea a court like the sons of Asherah."
19And Lady Asherah of the Sea replied:
20"Thou art great, O El, Thou are verily wise!
21The gray of Thy beard hath verily instructed Thee!
22Here are pectorals of gold for Thy breast.
23Lo, also it is the time of His rain.
24Baal sets the season,
25And gives forth His voice from the clouds.
26He flashes lightning to the earth.
27As a house of cedars let Him complete it,
28Or a house of bricks let Him erect it!
29Let it be told to Aliyan Baal: 'The mountains will bring Thee much silver.
30The hills, the choicest of gold;
31The mines will bring Thee precious stones,
32And build a house of silver and gold.
33A house of lapis gems!'"
34The Virgin Anath rejoices.
35She jumps with the feet
36And leaves the earth.
