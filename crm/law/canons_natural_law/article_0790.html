IV. Large Object Axioms
  
  4.3 Universal Objects  
  Article 79- Universe  
  Canon 665  
  The axioms within the Class of Natural Law of the Universe are:  
  1. The AXIOM CLASS of UNIVERSE is derived from the EXISTENCE of RULES and MATTER.  
  2. RULES are equivalent to the awareness of the UCA MODEL of three hundred and sixty (360) AXIOM SETS as defined here and the existence of MATTER.  
  3. MATTER is equivalent to the EXISTENCE of the UCA MODEL in some DIMENSION. Therefore the UNIVERSE is equivalent to OBJECTS in SPACE.  
  4. The UNIVERSE is equivalent to the UCA MODEL. Therefore the UNIVERSE is equivalent to UNIQUE COLLECTIVE AWARENESS. Therefore the UNIVERSE is equivalent to a DREAM.
