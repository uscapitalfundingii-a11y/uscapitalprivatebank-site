Ucadia Time

The TimeKeeper

The Office of TimeKeeper is a Supreme Office bound and bestowed to each Member of the Society and granted control to the Union Council as the Executive Government of the Globe Union Free Society to managed the official Time System of the Society.

The Concept of Time in culture is an extremely powerful notion, associated with symbols, rituals and traditions in many ways. When time is included in a religious faith or legal model, then such ideas can be extremely powerful, particularly when seeking to legally maintain power.

The concept of the TimeKeeper is therefore of extreme importance and a position now only found in a handful of Offices on the planet today, most notably the Supreme Pontiff, the Pope as Timekeeper of the Gregorian Calendar.

By claiming the role of TimeKeeper and "ownership" of time, through a system, a TimeKeeper may make such claims as to "suspend time", to "keep time", to "reverse time" and other spells.

Whilst such ideas may defy the laws of nature, the existence of such office, such powers nonetheless open up the ability to lawfully claim such fictional powers if no other system challenges such powers.

As much as a time system may be used as a source of power and control and as much as a TimeKeeper may enforce laws contrary to nature and the Divine, such inferior systems cannot stand against the power of a TimeKeeper of a time system based fundamentally on natural time and space.

Original Law referencing the existence and status of TimeKeeper

The Office of TimeKeeper is given reference, existence and status according to the Sacred and Divine Original Law De Pronuntionis Architectus Article X. The Office of TimeKeeper.

i. In recognition of our Blood Sovereign Right as the first and only true Timekeepers on behalf of the Divine Creator and in recognition of the sovereign rights and obligation of the Office of Architect as to the wish of the Divine Creator to re-establish the first and only true Time, the Office of Timekeeper is established. This Office shall be bound in perpetuity to the Office of Architect; and

ii. The Timekeeper shall record time for an on behalf of the Divine Creator for all life and forces of the Earth and the Solar System as the spiritual force of Unique Collective Awareness provides such extension by right to the whole Universe so that no other system or measurement of time shall be superior than the Time of UCADIA as issued and kept by the TimeKeeper; and

iii. The Timekeeper shall create an eight (8) part time system representing time for the Era, the Age, the Sun Year, the Solar season, the Moon Cycle, the Sun Arc, the Day of an Arc and the Name of the Day, the Hour and the Second/nano second; and

Office of TimeKeeper and Members of Globe Union Free Society

The Office of TimeKeeper and Architect is bound to the Office of Member of the Ucadia Society according to the Sacred and Divine Original Law De Pronuntionis Pactum Architectus Article XII. Transfer of Office of TimeKeeper.

Members agree to grant their collective rights to the Office of Members of the Union Council to wiself manage the Official Time System of the Society.

Inferior Time Systems and TimeKeepers

Other time systems being inferior to the UCADIA Time System have their own TimeKeeper. For example, the TimeKeeper of the Western Christian Time System known as the Gregorian Calendar is the Roman Pontiff.

However, unlike such time systems as the Roman Cult and the Roman Pontiff, the Official Time System of the Ucadia recognizes each and every member as being a TimeKeeper with the needs for common time managed centrally by the Executive of the Society.
