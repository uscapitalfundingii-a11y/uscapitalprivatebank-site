Q1. What is One-Heaven?  
  The Society of One-Heaven is a free society of united men, women and higher order spirits, living and deceased formed by sacred covenant known as Pactum De Singularis Caelum, also known as the Covenant of One Heaven.  
  As the first truly simultaneously spiritual and temporal society ever to exist on Earth, both the rules of membership and the benefits of membership extend not only across the whole Earth but Heaven as well.  
  Under the covenant of One Heaven, all men, women and higher order spirits that have ever existed and will ever exist are ipso facto (as a fact of law) members of One Heaven, whether they agree or disagree, consent or deny consent.  
  Furthermore, as the Covenant of One Heaven recognizes all men, woman and higher order spirits as Sovereign, a formal name of the Society of One Heaven which all lesser corporations must legally acknowledge is as The Kingdom of Heaven on Earth.  
   
Q2. Why the name? What does the name “One-Heaven” mean?  
  The name One-Heaven was chosen as a name that represents a central purpose of existence for the society—the uniting of Heaven as one.  
  It is a poorly understood fact that when people state a belief in Hell, they are implying that war still exists in the spiritual realm of heaven. As the Covenant of One Heaven ends all warfare through a series of historic and supremely sacred treaties, including the Treaty of Lucifer and the Treaty of Angels, Saints and Demons and the Treaty of Spirit States, the name was chosen to symbolically represent this unity instead of simply calling it the “heaven society” or some lesser name.  
   
Q3. What is the underlying idea underpinning the One-Heaven?  
  The underlying and central idea underpinning One-Heaven is that “you cannot have peace on Earth until you have peace in Heaven”—in other words, until a legitimate, respected document exists as proof that the war in Heaven is over and that all major religions see their central beliefs fulfilled, there can be no peace, nor guarantee of the future prosperity and survival of the Homo Sapien species.  
   
Q4. Who is really behind One-Heaven?  
  The Divine is behind the plan of One-Heaven, otherwise known by different religions as God, YHWH, Allah, The Great Power, the Universal Consciousness, the Unique Collective Awareness (UCA) and many other names. This is not an arrogant blasphemy, but a primary fact of religious law, secular law and scripture—every one of the major religions promise in their own way that One-Heaven will come into existence at an appointed time. This is that time.  
   
Q5. Who founded One-Heaven?, when and why?  
  A small group of Notary (Public and Private) founded One-Heaven Free Society of United Spirits with Frank O’Collins, the founder of UCADIA in Roman year 2009. The idea had existed for a few years earlier, but thanks to the involvement of the Notaries, the full realization of the importance of the Free Society in relation to the reestablishment of “rule of law” came to existence. The Notaries were able to explain that unless a Society is formed—superior in authority to the existing corrupted fictional laws that make every flesh and blood man and woman into “persons” (corporations)—there can be no justice, nor fairness. At the end of Roman Year 2010, it was agreed that all positions of One-Heaven must be now and forever purely spiritual and the Notaries disbanded their work down to local communities in anticipation of the Deeds of Trust as Covenants and Charters that re-convey and restore the rights of the flesh, the land and spirit to each and every man, woman and child granted by the Divine Creator through all sacred scripture to live together in freedom, without fear of debt or famine or war in each and every village, town, city and community.  
   
Q6. Who wrote Pactum De Singularis Caelum, the Covenant of One Heaven?  
  Frank O’Collins, with assistance of a team of Notaries wrote the first drafts of the Covenant with the final version of the Covenant (as is displayed on this site) confirmed by the Notary Conference by all Notaries Roman Date Thursday 17th December 2009 also known as UCA E8:Y3208:A0:S1:M2:D2.  
   
Q7. What are the main sources for the information in the Covenant of One-Heaven?  
  The primary sources for the content listed in the Covenant of One Heaven (Pactum De Singularis Caelum) come from three primary sources: (1) From the ancient scriptures and prophecies of the major religions of the world, especially Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Buddhism and Hindu; (2) From the UCADIAN Model and the inspired writing associated with the sovereign pronouncements of the Architect; and (3) Through inspiration, reflection and prayer upon the most sacred of scriptures of the major religions and the ideal course of events to end the war, bring peace and fulfilment to all members of the Society.

To determine this yourself and check off against the list of prophecies and scripture, you will need to read the whole of the Covenant of One Heaven as well as the background of Frank O’Collins as well as the UCADIAN Model.
