     Book of the Dead
 
  The Book of the Dead is defined by Article 37 of Pactum De Singularis Caelum (Covenant of One Heaven).  
  Only a corporation or fictional construct created by a corporation, having been issued a valid Certificate of Death may be entered into the one true and only Book of the Dead.
