II.   Divine Systems

2.4 Divine System of Spirit

Article 29 - SOL

Canon 235 (link)

SOL is the unique collective spirit of all sub atomic elements and awareness of the Solar System and Sun.

Canon 236 (link)

The spirit of SOL is part of the greater collective spirit of GAL, which is part of the greater collective spirit of UNITAS, which is part of the greater collective spirit of ALL.

Canon 237 (link)

SOL is the proper name and greater name of the Solar System in respect and recognition of the unified whole of all the planets, all the gases, all the moons, meteorites and asteroid belts, the Sun, all the life, all the self-aware life within the Solar System and the Solar System as one living entity.

Canon 238 (link)

SOL memory is the memory of the unique existence of the Solar System and memory of unique position, change, seasons of life and seasons of death. SOL memory is eternal.

Canon 239 (link)

The position of planets within the SOL is not simply mechanical, but will of the SOL. If the Sun chose it to be so, the planet Earth could be destroyed in an instant. Yet the Sun chooses of its own free will to value the life, awareness and existence of the planet Earth like a mother to a new born child.

Canon 240 (link)

As SOL continues to demonstrate active intelligent will to protect the Earth from great harm, any belief system founded on rules and laws implying negative intent upon the Earth by the Solar System are false and null and void ab initio (from the beginning).
