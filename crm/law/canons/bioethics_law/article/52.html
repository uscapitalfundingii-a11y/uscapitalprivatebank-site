II.   Life

2.5 Medical Treatment

Article 52 - Pharmaceuticals

Canon 4905 (link)

A Pharmaceutical is a natural or artificial substance or compound manufactured for use as a medicinal drug.

Canon 4906 (link)

The word pharmaceutic and pharmaceutical comes from Ancient Greek word pharmaxeno meaning “to practice witchcraft” and pharmaxon meaning “poison”. Hence pharmaxeno is equivalent to the Latin medicina.

Canon 4907 (link)

Unlike Therapeutic practices using drugs, pharmaceutic substances have traditionally been founded and obsessed on the occult art and use of poisons.

Canon 4908 (link)

To discredit the therapeutic arts and knowledge of naturally grown drugs, the Roman Cult in the 14th Century introduced the commercial licensing model of apothecaries whereby one was not permitted to prepare or offer natural herbs and drugs unless one was a member of the private guild of apothecaries.

Canon 4909 (link)

Unlike therapeutic healers that offered herbs and drugs to heal, members of the occult apothecary guild were trained in pharmaceutics being the mixing of poisons to produce placebo effects and temporary relief to genuine herbal remedies, thus seeking to marginalize genuine natural remedies.

Canon 4910 (link)

In order to further corrupt historic knowledge of genuine herbal and natural drug cures, the Magyar - Khazar overlords created the entirely fictitious pseudo-science called Alchemy in the 14th Century including a host of fake texts and references to it. However, by the 18th Century after only two hundred (200) years, Alchemy had largely been repudiated, except the elite who even into the 20th Century believed their own propaganda to be true.

Canon 4911 (link)

The alleged text called De Materia Medica as a claimed Latin translation from Arabic of some ancient Greek texts by a 1st Century therapeutic called Pedanius Dioscorides is a horrible and absurd 16th Century fake created to deliberately discredit ancient knowledge of natural herbs and drugs in order to promote and solidify the Roman Cult stranglehold on remedy and cure.

Canon 4912 (link)

By the late 19th Century, the apothecary guild had given way to state based pharmacy guilds and the introduction of the chemist, also known as the pharmacist in light of new breakthroughs in the commercial mass production in the synthesizing of compounds.

Canon 4913 (link)

By the late 19th Century, those apothecaries that did not choose to migrate to the commercial world of chemists and pharmacists became known as homeopaths. Today, the homeopathy industry is falsely aligned to “natural therapy” rather than its root as pure pharmaceutics.

Canon 4914 (link)

By the 1st decades of the 20th Century, commercial synthesizing of compounds that mimicked naturally grown narcotics spurred the movement by the elite to make various forms of naturally grown and free narcotics illegal, while controlling the manufacture and distribution of synthetic pain killers.

Canon 4915 (link)

Consistent with the ancient right of all civilizations since the beginning of time, all laws, controls, edicts, threats and regulations that ban the growth and distribution of naturally grown narcotics are hereby null and void from the beginning.

Canon 4916 (link)

Consistent with ending the corruption of the world by the elite Magyar - Khazar families for their own ends, any official that refuses to repeal, or end the prohibition of the free growth of naturally grown narcotics is guilty of a crime against humanity and all civilization.

Canon 4917 (link)

The synthesizing, manufacture and distribution of synthetic pain killers by pharmaceutical entities and any derivative is hereby forbidden, reprobate, suppressed and not permitted to be revived.
