II.   Life

2.2 Genetics

Article 32 - Genome as Property

Canon 4792 (link)

The Genome and specific genetic material of certain species as a form of Property is present in a range of commercial claims and laws since the 20th Century.

Canon 4793 (link)

Under Western Law, Islamic Law, Talmudic Law, Hindu Law, Buddhist Law and most advanced Indigenous law systems, the Divine Creator is respected and recognized as the ultimate creator and absolute owner of all life. Therefore, by all civilized law it is the Divine Creator that is the absolute original owner of all genetic material and no other.

Canon 4794 (link)

As all forms of civilized law recognize the Divine Creator as the absolute owner of all life, any claim of absolute title or ownership over any genetic material by any entity other than the Divine Creator is a fundamental breach of the very nature of Property and law itself.

Canon 4795 (link)

All men and women are bestowed by our Divine Creator, also known as Unique Collective Awareness (UCA), fixed and irrevocable title over their body, mind and spirit. Therefore, this includes fixed and irrevocable title over all the genetic material within their bodies and that no force may lawfully abrogate any of these rights, with or without consent.

Canon 4796 (link)

Any false claim or law that grants part ownership of genetic material shared by more than one (1) man or woman is therefore in direct violation of the fixed and irrevocable title of all other men and women over their bodies, minds and spirits.

Canon 4797 (link)

All patents granting rights and ownership based on genetic material are founded on fraud, are an abomination against the very laws of the society that falsely granted such patents and are therefore hereby null and void from the beginning, with no force or effect.
