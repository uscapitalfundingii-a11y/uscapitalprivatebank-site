I.   Introductory provisions

1.2 Concepts

Article 17 - Mutation

Canon 4723 (link)

Mutation is a term used to describe small or large changes in the genomic sequence of RNA or DNA thereby producing a change in the physical structure, function or performance of an organism.

Canon 4724 (link)

Mutations may be broadly defined into two categories being Micro and Macro:

(i) Micro Mutations are changes to part of a gene or its associated programming sequence, producing a modification in the performance of any proteins produced by the gene; and

(ii) Macro Mutations are large changes to a whole gene, or entire sequence resulting in new genes, the cessation of function of a gene or even a new expression of a chromosome (large sequence of DNA stored separately from other sequences).

Canon 4725 (link)

The catalysts for Micro Mutations include radiation, viruses, chemicals as well as transcription errors during meiosis or DNA replication.

Canon 4726 (link)

Contrary to Micro Mutations, the catalysts for Macro Mutations are far more limited including extreme radiation, significant chemical change and genetic engineering known as “Alteration”.

Canon 4727 (link)

As Macro Mutation fundamentally alters the basic function of entire genes or even chromosomes, significant study of the effect of Macro Mutations and DNA have proven that most organisms fail to survive or reproduce. When such deadly Macro Mutation affects an entire species it is called an Extinction Mutation.

Canon 4728 (link)

Given the propensity for Macro Mutations to cause the death of a particular species, the fossil and biological record proves that most species undergo no more than one (1) or two (2) Macro Mutation every few hundred thousand years.

Canon 4729 (link)

It is statistically impossible for a species to undergo three (3) or more “naturally occurring” Macro Mutations within the space of a few hundred thousand years and not result in extinction.

Canon 4730 (link)

It is statistically impossible and absurd to conclude that a species may survive one (1) Extinction Mutation without external assistance for its continued survival.

Canon 4731 (link)

The species of Homo Sapiens is the product of no less than seven (7) Macro Mutations which are also Extinction Mutations that occurred approximately one hundred eighty thousand (180,000) to two hundred thousand (200,000) years ago:

(i) Fused X1 and X2 sex chromosomes to produce forty six (46) chromosomes instead of forty eight (48) chromosomes for all other species of the Hominidae family on planet Earth; and

(ii) The constant risk of choking on food as a result of the larynx (voice box) situated much lower than in all other primates with the epiglottis incapable of reaching the roof of the mouth, with the teeth pushed forward and compressed, massively increasing risk of disease and infection; and

(iii) The need to chew food longer than any other primate rather than swallow instantaneously or risk choking to death, dramatically increasing the time needed to eat; and

(iv) The thinnest and worst repairing skin of any primate or mammal on planet Earth meaning in the absence of knowledge of civilization the species should not have survived; and

(v) The absence of hair in contrast to all other above ground mammals that when combined with the poor repairing of skin and in the absence of knowledge of civilization the species should not have survived; and

(vi) The boneless penis in contrast with every other mammal on planet Earth requiring a much larger time needed to copulate than another other species; and

(vii) The giant infant head of between 350-450cc compared to a reduced birth canal on account of repositioned hips for bipedal walking, resulting in extreme pain during child birth and the real risk of fatal blood loss due to tissue and blood vessel damage meaning in the absence of knowledge of civilization the species should not have survived; and

Canon 4732 (link)

When taken as a whole, it is both statistically impossible and the height of absurdity to suggest the Macro Mutation and Extinction Mutations that produced the Homo Sapien species occurred naturally. Therefore, the only explanation is alteration by an alien species with capable technology for advanced genetic engineering.
