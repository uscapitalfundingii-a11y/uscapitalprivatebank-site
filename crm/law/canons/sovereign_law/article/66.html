II.   Sovereign

2.6 Holly Roman Law Form

Article 66 - Pappas (Pope)

Canon 5861 (link)

Pappas, also known as Pappa, also known as Pope was the official title of the ruler and leader of the Holly Roman Empire originally created from its foundation under Constantine in 326 CE until its effective collapse at the destruction of Constantinople in 1096.

Canon 5862 (link)

The term pappas (φαφφας) in Ancient Greek means “father” and was chosen in honor of the implementation as law the sacred scripture of the Gnostics and Nazarene teachings of Holly Irish Prince “Esus” through the first true Christian Bible known as the Bibiliographe, or βιβλιογράφη in 326 CE.

Canon 5863 (link)

To continue his destruction of all the core elements of the pagan Roman Empire, Constantine commissioned a sacred oath to the ruler of the Holly Roman Empire, known as the Nicene Creed. While the original text has been deliberately destroyed, the original form was similar to the following:

Our Father, Holly (Sacred) is your name,

May your kingdom keep and your will be done,

On Earth as it ordained in Heaven.

Give us this day our daily needs,

And forgive us for our transgressions,

As we forgive those who transgress against us,

But protect us from all evil.

Canon 5864 (link)

In the 8th Century, the Carolingians deliberately corrupted this oath to the ruler of the Holly Roman Empire into “the Lord’s Prayer” as a means of diminishing the authority of the Imperial Christian Pope.

Canon 5865 (link)

In the 12th Century, the Roman Cult claimed the title of Pappas (Pope) for themselves, without any valid provenance or legitimacy and after being directly responsible for the destruction of Constantinople and Imperial Christian Church.

Canon 5866 (link)

In the 16th Century, the Roman Cult deliberately corrupted the Carolingian version of the Pappas Oath (Lord’s Prayer) back to being an oath to the Roman Cult Pope by declaring the Vatican and Holy See to be a “supernatural kingdom”.

Canon 5867 (link)

As the Roman Cult, also known as the Vatican is directly responsible for the destruction of Constantinople in 1096 CE and the Imperial Christian Church, all claims of right to use Pappas, Pappa or Pope as a title are therefore false, an abomination against all Christian teachings and a complete fraud.

Canon 5868 (link)

As the Roman Cult has absolutely no legitimacy or right to claim the title of Pappas, Pappa or Pope, the Lord’s Prayer provides absolutely no protection or validity to the existence or claims of the Vatican as either a spiritual or temporal state.
