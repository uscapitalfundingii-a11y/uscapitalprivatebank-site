IV.   Faith

4.1 Cosmology

Article 99 - Atheology

Canon 4147 (link)

Atheology is any Cosmology model based on the presumption that a single universal Supreme Being cannot or does not exist. The religion of Scientism, also known as Science is based on Atheology.

Canon 4148 (link)

Unlike other types of Cosmology models, Atheology is founded on the absolute presumption of the denial that a Supreme Being exists, or is necessary.

Canon 4149 (link)

Atheology is characterized as a fanatical model in that it is proven to selectively deny elements that do not fit its cosmology model, while maintaining the false premise that it is the most accurate and objective model.

Canon 4150 (link)

As the canons of Natural Law prove the existence of Unique Collective Awareness and therefore a Supreme Divine Creator, Atheology is an inferior Cosmology.

Canon 4151 (link)

As the canons of Natural Law prove the existence of Unique Collective Awareness and therefore a Supreme Divine Creator, all Theologies founded on Atheology are by definition inferior.
