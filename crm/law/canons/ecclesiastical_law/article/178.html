V.   Sacraments

5.4 Apostolic Sacraments

Article 178 - Veneration

Canon 4537 (link)

Veneration is the twelth of the twelve (12) Apostolic Life Sacraments also known as “The Twelve”, “The Twelve Apostles” and “The Means”. Veneration is granted and administered by a valid Minister following death and the sacrament of Remembrance in accordance with these canons and associated approved liturgy.

Canon 4538 (link)

The purpose of the sacrament of Veneration is a formal rite of blessing and remembrance to an incumbent in accordance with these canons and associated approved liturgy. The meaning of Veneration is derived from the Latin word venere/venert meaning “to regard with respect, reverence, or heartfelt deference”.

Canon 4539 (link)

The sacrament of Veneration comprises the completion of three separate and deliberate Acts in order: Act One being the Act of Conception, Act Two being the Act of Inception and Act Three being the Act of Perfection.
