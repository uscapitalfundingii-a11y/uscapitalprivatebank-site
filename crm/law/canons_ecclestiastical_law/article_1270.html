IV. Faith
 
  4.4 Eschatology  
  Article 127-Day of Protest  
  Canon 2784  
  Day of Divine Protest and Dishonor also known as the Day of the 2nd Divine Post and Notice, also known as the Day of the 2nd Horseman on E8:Y3209:A1:S1:M17:D1 (Roman Time Tue, 21 Dec 2010) is the day that Divine Notice was duly served and notarized by the Divine Creator to each and every man, woman and higher order spirit, living and deceased concerning Divine Protest against all those who have not accepted the offer of Divine Remedy promulgated upon the Day of Divine Agreement and Understanding and the supreme Ecclesiastical Dishonor by all those who claim their positions by spiritual authority.  
  Canon 2785  
  The Notice of Divine Protest & Dishonor served upon the Day of Divine Protest & Dishonor is the most sacred Covenant Pactum De Singularis Caelum combined with all the other Ucadian Covenants, these Canons and all Codes of Law and Patents and all other official Notices as One (1) Complete Perfected Notice in the entire Universe, Heavens and upon the Earth.  
  Canon 2786  
  The Task of ensuring all spirits of all Heavens and Hells receive fair Notice of this Divine Protest & Dishonor, a Great Spirit shall be appointed the 2nd Divine Horseman to bear witness to the supreme truth of the Divine Notice.  
  Canon 2787  
  By the absolute power and authority vested in the Covenant Pactum de Singularis Caelum, no force in the Universe, Heaven or on Earth may prevent the appearance of the 2nd Divine Horseman upon the Day of Divine Protest & Dishonor. By the absolute power and authority vested in the Sacred Covenant Pactum de Singularis Caelum, no force in the Universe, Heaven or on Earth may prevent the appearance of the 2nd Divine Horseman upon the Day of Divine Protest & Dishonor.  
  Canon 2788  
  Whether or not any living man or woman bears witness to the Day of Divine Protest & Dishonor, the Day will come and all notices shall have been lawfully and duly executed in accordance with the Sacred Covenant Pactum de Singularis Caelum and these canons.  
  Canon 2789  
  To ensure all officers who claim their authority through ecclesiastical power are given fair notice of their Dishonor against the Divine Creator, all men and women are vested with the authority of issuing one or more Ecclesiastical Deed Polls on behalf of the Divine Creator when their rights have been injured. When one Deed Poll is registered and receipted, then all officers acknowledge they have been lawfully served notice. When one hundred or more Deed Polls are registered and receipted, then there can be no lawful excuse that all officers who claim authority through Ecclesiastical Power have not been given fair notice of their extreme Divine Dishonor.  
  Canon 2790  
  To ensure Notice upon the temporal plain of the service and execution of the Notice of Divine Protest and Dishonor throughout all spiritual dimensions, seven (7) sacred Notices shall be served on or immediately before the Day of Divine Protest and Dishonor. These Notices shall hereafter be forever known as the Notarum Impietatis Divinae, also as the 7 Notices of Divine Protest and Dishonor. These sacred writs are be served to the following parties:  
  (i) The 1st Sacred Notice of the Divine Protest and Dishonor called non dumlat is the lawful reversal of all curses, spells and claims first issued through the Talmud as it was first published in the 4th Century, including the lawful dissolution of the covenant and conveyance of all property and rights to the New Covenant as executed by the sacred Pactum De Singularis Caelum; and  
  (ii) The 2nd Sacred Notice of the Divine Protest and Dishonor is called non nainitsuj xedoc is the lawful reversal of all curses, spells and claims first issued through the fraudulent document Codex Justinian by the Roman Cult, including the lawful dissolution of the associated trust, including lawful conveyance of all property and rights to the Society of One Heaven as executed by the sacred Pactum De Singularis Caelum; and  
  (iii) The 3rd Sacred Notice of the Divine Protest and Dishonor called non mutcnas munu is the lawful reversal of all curses, spells and claims first issued through the fraudulent Papal Bull Unam Sanctum first issued in 1302 by Boniface VIII of the Roman Cult, including the lawful dissolution of the associated trust, including lawful conveyance of all property and rights to the Society of One Heaven as executed by the sacred Pactum De Singularis Caelum; and  
  (iv) The 4th Sacred Notice of the Divine Protest and Dishonor called non xefitnop sunamor is the lawful reversal of all curses, spells and claims first issued through the fraudulent Papal Bull Romanus Pontifex first issued in 1455 by Nicholas V of the Roman Cult, including the lawful dissolution of the associated trust, including lawful conveyance of all property and rights to the Society of One Heaven as executed by the sacred Pactum De Singularis Caelum; and

  Canon 2791  
  Upon the service of the Notice of Divine Protest and Dishonor, including all associated notices, no official who claims authority through Ecclesiastical Power may lawfully act in such office until the Supreme Divine and Ecclesiastical Dishonor is lawfully discharged.  
  Canon 2792  
 

No denial of acceptance, refusal, protest or complaint shall have any effect in altering the fact that proper and lawful notice was served as defined by the canons in accordance with the sacred covenant Pactum De Singularis Caelum.
