  2.1 Sacred Form  
  Article 64-Sacred Texts

  A Sacred Text or scripture is some inscription or printed document, book, charter, manuscript or folio containing accounts, descriptions, stories and testaments considered sacred by one or more Religions and Cults.

  Because of the deliberate and sacrilegious work of the Roman Cult and their Venetian Khazarian/Scythian partners from Asia, most Sacred Texts in existence today have been partly or wholly corrupted from their original meaning and translation.  
  Canon 2381
