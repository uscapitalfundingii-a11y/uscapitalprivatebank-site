  3.3 Holiness  
  Article 79-Holy  
  Canon 2484  
  The word Holy is derived from the 15th century word hallow, itself from the 11th Century Saxon word halig meaning to make holly, to make sacred; to consecrate, set apart a person or object as sacred; to bless and venerate, respect as the highest to the Divine Creator. Hence, Holy from hallow is both the ritual of making sacred and the claimed Divine authority and power for such ritual.  
  Canon 2485  
  The word Holy is closely related to the word Holly, from which it is ultimately derived. The difference being Holy is the ritual of making an object or concept sacred, whereas Holly is already a sacred person, concept or object. The difference was deliberately obscured in the 16th Century so that the word Holy is incorrectly and mistakenly used to describe both ritual and quality itself.
