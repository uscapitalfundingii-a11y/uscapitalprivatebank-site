VII. Sacraments
 
  7.1 Life Sacraments  
  Article 156-Bel Espirit  
  Canon 2934  
  Bel Espirit is one of the twelve (12) Life Sacraments. Bel Espirit is granted and administered within 30 days of the 21st birthday of a young adult in accordance with these canons and associated approved liturgy  
  Canon 2935  
  The sacrament of Bel Espirit is named in honor of the French bel espirit meaning “fine mind”. It is the celebration of the point of transition from Adolescence to Youthhood.  
  Canon 2936  
  A condition of Bel Espirit is that the youth knowingly and willingly consents to dedicating their life to continuous self improvement and virtue and to contributing to the benefit and improvement of their community and society.  
  Canon 2937  
  In accordance with Divine Law and the most sacred covenant Pactum De Singularis Caelum, if a teenager having received the sacrament of Adventus dies before being the age of twenty one (21), then this unique Divine Immortal Spirit shall be fully entitled to receive the sacrament of Bel Espirit within 30 days of 21 years since being borne and all Life Sacraments thereafter at their appointed time.  
  Canon 2938  
  In accordance with Divine Law and the most sacred covenant Pactum De Singularis Caelum, the mother or father or next of living kin of any child that died prior to the age of twenty-one (21) yet was not previously granted the sacrament of Adventus may apply for the special ceremony of Life Sacraments where all sacraments are granted beginning with Annunciation to the sacrament representing the same age as if the child had lived and grown up to the present day.
