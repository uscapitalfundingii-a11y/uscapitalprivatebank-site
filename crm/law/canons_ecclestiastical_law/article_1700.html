VII. Sacraments
  
  7.2 Society Sacraments  
  Article 170-Foundation  
  Canon 3001  
  Foundation is one of the thirteen (13) Society Sacraments. Foundation is granted and administered at the consecration and dedication of the foundation stone or plaque to a building or location in accordance with these canons and associated approved liturgy.  
  Canon 3002  
  No actual or simulated form of sacrifice is permitted to be associated with a Foundation sacrament, nor is the use of deceased animal flesh, blood or bone of any kind permitted to be involved.
