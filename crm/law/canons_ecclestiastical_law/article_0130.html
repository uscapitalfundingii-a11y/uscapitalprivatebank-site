I. Introductory Provisions
  
  1.2 Concepts  
  Article 13-Clergy  
  Canon 2009  
  Clergy is a term used to describe a body of Ministers, who are trained and ordained for religious service within a particular Religion or Cult.  
  Canon 2010  
  Just as all spiritual dimensions including Heaven, Purgatory and Hell constitute one college as the Society of One Heaven, so in a like manner all Clergy of all valid Religions and Cults are united amongst themselves in accordance with these canons.  
  Canon 2011  
  The Apostolic Prothonotaries of the Sacred Rota and Supreme Court of One Heaven and Apostolic Prothorabban of the Divine Sanhedrin, in whom continues the offices and powers given by Divine Decree and most Sacred of all ancient Covenants, are the highest and most senior Clergy of all and the pastors of all Religions and Cults upon the Earth and Solar System. By virtue of their office, they possess supreme, full, immediate and universal power over all Religions and Cults, which they are able to exercise freely.  
  Canon 2012  
  No appeal or recourse is permitted against a sentence or decree of the Apostolic Prothonotaries of the Sacred Rota and Supreme Court of One Heaven.  
  Canon 2013  
  No office of any Religion or Cult may claim higher authority than the Supreme Court of One Heaven and the Apostolic Prothorabban. Any document which contradicts this canon is null and void having no legal effect to the extent of such errors having no force or law.
