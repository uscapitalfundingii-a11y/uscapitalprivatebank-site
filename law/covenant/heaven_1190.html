XI. Procedures
  
  Article 119 - Guardian Spirits  
119.1 Guardian Spirits  
  It is the deepest belief and hope of all souls that in honouring our ancestors and saints we shall be granted guardian spirits to watch over and protect us from harm.
 
  And so it shall be by this Covenant one of the primary objectives of Heaven that departed souls are trained and enter into the service of guardian to a living soul or souls for the duration of their lives.
 
  Where insufficient departed souls are available for one to one guardianship, it shall be a primary objective of this Covenant to see that all living men and women have at least some primary protection on a regular basis.
