VII. Sacraments
 
  7.1 Life Sacraments  
  Article 161-Resolution  
  Canon 2954  
  Resolution is one of the twelve (12) Life Sacraments. Resolution is granted and administered within one day of imminent death in accordance with these canons and associated approved liturgy.  
  Canon 2955  
  The sacrament of Resolution is named in honor of the Latin resolvere meaning “to untie, to dissolve”.  
  Canon 2956  
  Resolution is the special celebration of blessing and cleansing to help an individual in the final stages of death to find a point of peace and resolution to their life in the hope of evoking the care of other minds already crossed to help guide the person safely.
