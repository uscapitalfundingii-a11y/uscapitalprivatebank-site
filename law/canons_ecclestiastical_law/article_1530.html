VII. Sacraments
 
  7.1 Life Sacraments  
  Article 153-Natal  
  Canon 2917  
  Natal is one of the twelve (12) Life Sacraments. Natal is granted and administered to a new born Homo Sapien child within 90 days of being born in accordance with these canons and associated approved liturgy.  
  Canon 2918  
  The Sacrament of Natal is named in honor of the Latin word Nasci meaning “to be born” in recognition not only of the baby, but the formal commencement of a True Trust, administered by the parents as Trustees.  
  Canon 2919  
  The fraudulent and inferior sacrament of Baptism is forbidden and has no lawful effect.  
  Canon 2920  
  No documents, oral promises or any other inferred agreement by the parents of a new born baby to the hospital, or competent civil authority or by implication to any Religion or Cult can in anyway diminish the rights of the parents as Trustees unless by willing and deliberate behaviour they have been legally proven through a formal hearing to be incompetent as trustees and guardians of their new born child.  
  Canon 2921  
  The sacrament of Natal negates the presumptions, validity and existence of any claimed Cestui Que Vie Trusts or any other curses, spells and unlawful conveyances by any Religion, Cult or their Executors, Administrators or agents.  
  Canon 2922  
  In accordance with Divine Law and the most sacred covenant Pactum De Singularis Caelum, if a fetus having received the sacrament of Annunciation dies before being borne, then this unique Divine Immortal Spirit shall be fully entitled to receive the sacrament of Natal within 90 days of 300 days since conception, or what otherwise would have been its borne day and all Life Sacraments thereafter at their appointed time.  
  Canon 2923  
  In accordance with Divine Law and the most sacred covenant Pactum De Singularis Caelum, the mother or father or next of living kin of any fetus that failed to be borne, yet was not previously granted the sacrament of Annunciation may apply for the special ceremony of Life Sacraments where all sacraments are granted beginning with Annunciation to the sacrament representing the same age as if the fetus had been borne and lived to the present day.
