Chapter - 2

1The lads depart, they do not Sit.
2Then They set face toward the Mountain of Night, toward the Convocation of the Assembly.
3The Gods had not even sat down, the Deities to dine, when Baal stood up by El.
4As soon as the Gods saw Them, saw the messengers of Yam, the emissaries of Judge Nahar, the Gods lowered Their heads upon Their knees.
5Yea, upon the thrones of Their lordships.
6Baal rebukes Them: "Why, O Gods, have Ye lowered, your heads on top of Your knees, yea, upon the thrones of Your lordships?
7Let a pair of Gods read the tablets of the messengers of Yam, of the emissaries of Judge Nahar!
8O Gods, lift up Your heads from the top of Your knees yea, from the thrones of Your lordships!
9And I shall answer the messengers of Yam, the emissaries of Judge Nahar!"
10The Gods lift Their heads from the top of their knees yea, from the thrones of their lordships.
11After there arrive the messengers of Yam, the emissaries of JudgeNahar.
12At the feet of El They do not fall, they do not prostrate themselves before the Convocation of the Assembly.
13Arise, for they declare their information.
14A fire, two fires!
15He sees a burnished sword!
16They say to The Bull, His father, El: "The message of Yam, Your lord, of Your master, Judge Nahar:
17'Give up, O Gods, Him whom Ye harbor, him whom the multitudes harbor!
18Give up Baal and His partisans, Dagon's Son, so that I may inherit His gold!'"
19And The Bull, His father, El, replies: "Baal is Thy slave, O Yam!
20Baal is Thy slave O Yam!
21Dagon's Son is Thy captive!
22He will bring Thy tribute like the Gods.
23Like the Deities, Thy gift!"
24But Prince Baal was infuriated.
25A knife He takes in the hand, a dagger in the right hand.
26To smite the lads He flourishes it.
27Anath siezes His right hand, Astarte seizes His left hand: "How canst Thou smite the messengers of Yam? The emissaries of Judge Nahar?
28They have merely brought the words of Yam-Nahar, word of Their Lord and Master."
29But Prince Baal is infuriated.
30He spares the lives of the messengers; He sends Them back to Their master.
31He instructs them to give His information: Baal will not bow to Prince Yam.
32He will not be the slave of Judge Nahar.
33He declares once more that he shall slay the Tyrant lord of the Gods.
34"To the earth let Our mighty one fall!
35Yea, to dust Our strong one!"
