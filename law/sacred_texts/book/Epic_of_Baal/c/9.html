Chapter - 9

1Kothar-u-Khasis laughed.
2He lifts His voice And shouts: "Did I not tell Thee, O Aliyan Baal,
3That Thou wouldst return, Baal, to My word?
4Let a casement be opened in the house,
5A window in the midst of the palace!"
6Baal opened the clouds with rain, His holy voice He gives forth in the heavens.
7The enemies of Baal seize the forests, The foes of Hadad, the fringes of the mountain.
8And Aliyan Baal declares: "Enemies of Hadad, why do Ye invade?
9Why do Ye invade the arsenal of Our defense?"
10Weeping, Baal returns to His house: "Whether king Or commander Be invested with sovereignty over the land,
11Respects I shall not send to Mavet, Nor greetings to El's beloved, the Hero!"
12Mavet calls from His throat, The Beloved meditates in His inwards: "I alone am He who will rule over the Gods.
13Yea command Gods and men.
14Even dominate the multitudes of the earth."
15Aloud Baal cries to His lads: "Look, Gupan and Ugar, sons of Galmat, Errand lads, sons of Zalmat The lofty and distinguished!
16Then surely set face Toward the mountain of Tergezz,
17Toward the mountain of Shermeg,
18Toward the furrow of the thriving of the earth.
19Lift the mountain on the hands,
20The hill on top of the palms,
21And go down into to nether-reaches of the earth
22So that You will be counted amoung those who go down into the earth!
23Then shall Ye set face Toward His city, Hemry.
24Lo, the throne on which He sits In the midst of the land of His inheritance And the guards of the defense of the Gods.
25Do not draw near the God Mavet, Lest He make You like a lamb in His mouth,
26Like a kid in His jaws Ye be crushed!
27The Torch of the Gods, Shapash, burns;
28The heavens halt on account of El's darling, Mavet.
29By the thousand acres,
30Yea the myriad hectares
31At the feet of Mavet bow and fall.
32Prostrate Yourselves and honor Him!
33And say to the God Mavet,
34Declare to El's beloved, the Hero:
35And Baal spoke His word to His lads.
36He sent His message to Mavet.
37The Lord Hadad refused to pay tribute to the Beloved of El.
38Mavet was enfuriated, and sent His word back to Baal.
39He declared that, because Baal had destroyed the Serpent Lotan, He would exact revenge by devouring Baal.
40The messengers of Baal informed Baal that Mavet would open His mouth wide.
41"A lip to earth,
42A lip to heaven,
43And a tounge to the stars
44So that Baal may enter His inwards,
45Yea, descend into His mouth
46As scorched is the olive,
47The produce of the Earth,
48And the fruit of the Trees."
49Aliyan Baal fears Him,
50The Rider of the Clouds dreads Him.
51"Depart! Speak to the God Mavet.
52Declare to El's Beloved, the Hero:
53The message of Aliyan Baal,
54The word of Aliy the Warrior: 'Hail, O God Mavet!
