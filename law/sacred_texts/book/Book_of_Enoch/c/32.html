Chapter - 32

1After this I saw another dream, and explained it all to you, my son.
2Enoch arose and said to his son Mathusala, To you, my son, will I speak. Hear my word; and incline your ear to the visionary dream of your father.
3Before I married your mother Edna, I saw a vision on my bed;
4And behold, a cow sprung forth from the earth;
5And this cow was white.
6Afterwards a female heifer sprung forth; and with it another heifer: one of them was black, and one was red.
7The black heifer then struck the red one, and pursued it over the earth.
8From that period I could see nothing more of the red heifer; but the black one increased in bulk, and a female heifer came with him.
9After this I saw that many cows proceeded forth, resembling him, and following after him.
10The first female young one also went out in the presence of the first cow; and sought the red heifer, but found him not.
11And she lamented with a great lamentation, while she was seeking him.
12Then I looked until that first cow came to her, from which time she became silent, and ceased to lament.
13Afterwards she calved another white cow.
14And again calved many cows and black heifers.
15In my sleep also I perceived a white bull, which in like manner grew, and became a large white bull.
16After him many white cows came forth, resembling him.
17And they began to calve many other white cows, which resembled them and followed each other.
18Again I looked attentively, while sleeping, and surveyed heaven above.
19And behold a single star fell from heaven.
20Which being raised up, ate and fed among those cows.
21After that I perceived other large and black cows; and behold all of them changed their stalls and pastures, while their young began to lament one with another.
22Again I looked in my vision, and surveyed heaven; when behold I saw many stars which descended, and projected themselves from heaven to where the first star was, into the midst of those young ones; while the cows were with them, feeding in the midst of them.
23I looked at and observed them; when behold, they all acted after the manner of horses, and began to approach the young cows, all of whom became pregnant, and brought forth elephants, camels, and asses.
24At these all the cows were alarmed and terrified; when they began biting with their teeth, swallowing, and striking with their horns.
25They began also to devour the cows; and behold all the children of the earth trembled, shook with terror at them, and suddenly fled away.
26Again I perceived them, when they began to strike and to swallow each other; and the earth cried out.
27Then I raised my eyes a second time towards heaven, and saw in a vision, that, behold, there came forth from heaven as it were the likeness of white men. One came forth from thence, and three with him.
28Those three, who came forth last, seized me by my hand; and raising me up from the generations of the earth, elevated me to a high station.
29Then they showed me a lofty tower on the earth, while every hill became diminished.
30And they said, Remain here, until you perceive what shall come upon those elephants, camels, and asses, upon the stars, and upon all the cows.
31Then I looked at that one of the four white men, who came forth first.
32He seized the first star which fell down from heaven.
33And, binding it hand and foot, he cast it into a valley; a valley narrow, deep, stupendous, and gloomy.
34Then one of them drew his sword, and gave it to the elephants, camels, and asses, who began to strike each other. And the whole earth shook on account of them.
35And when I looked in the vision, behold, one of those four angels, who came forth, hurled from heaven, collected together, and took all the great stars, whose form partly resembles that of horses; and binding them all hand and foot, cast them into the cavities of the earth.
36Then one of those four went to the white cows, and taught them a mystery. While the cow was trembling, it was born, and became a man, and fabricated for himself a large ship. In this he dwelt, and three cows dwelt with him in that ship, which covered them.
37Again I lifted up my eyes towards heaven, and saw a lofty roof. Above it were seven cataracts, which poured fourth on a certain village much water.
38Again I looked, and behold there were fountains open on the earth in that large village.
39The water began to boil up, and rose over the earth; so that the village was not seen, while its whole soil was covered with water.
40Much water was over it, darkness, and clouds. Then I surveyed the height of this water; and it was elevated above the village.
41It flowed over the village, and stood higher than the earth.
42Then all the cows which were collected there, while I looked on them, were drowned, swallowed up, and destroyed in the water.
43But the ship floated above it. All the cows, the elephants, the camels, and the asses, were drowned on the earth, and all cattle.
44Nor could I perceive them. Neither were they able to get out, but perished, and sunk into the deep.
45Again I looked in the vision until those cataracts from that lofty roof were removed, and the fountains of the earth became equalized, while other depths were opened;
46Into which the water began to descend, until the dry ground appeared.
47The ship remained on the earth; the darkness receded; and it became light.
48Then the white cow, which became a man, went out of the ship, and the three cows with him.
49One of the three cows was white, resembling that cow; one of them was red as blood; and one of them was black. And the white cow left them.
50Of all these the different kinds assembled together, lions, tigers, wolves, dogs, wild boars, foxes, rabbits, and the hanzar.
51The siset, the avest, kites, the phonkas, and ravens.
52Then the white cow was born in the midst of them.
53And they began to bite each other; when the white cow, which was born in the midst of them, brought forth a wild ass and a white cow at the same time, and after that many wild asses.
54Then the white cow, which was born, brought forth a black wild sow and a white sheep.
55That wild sow also brought forth many swine.
56And that sheep brought forth twelve sheep.
57When those twelve sheep grew up, they delivered one of them to the asses.
58Again those asses delivered that sheep to the wolves,
59And he grew up in the midst of them.
60Then the Lord brought the eleven other sheep, that they might dwell and feed with him in the midst of the wolves.
61They multiplied, and there was abundance of pasture for them.
62But the wolves began to frighten and oppress them, while they destroyed their young ones.
63And they left their young in torrents of deep water.
64Now the sheep began to cry out on account of their young, and fled for refuge to their Lord. One however, which was saved, escaped, and went away to the wild asses.
65I beheld the sheep moaning, crying, and petitioning their Lord.
66With all their might, until the Lord of the sheep descended at their voice from his lofty habitation; went to them; and inspected them.
67He called to that sheep which had secretly stolen away from the wolves, and told him to make the wolves understand that they were not to touch the sheep.
68Then that sheep went to the wolves with the word of the Lord, when another met him, and proceeded with him.
69Both of them together entered the dwelling of the wolves; and conversing with them made them understand, that thenceforwards they were not to touch the sheep.
70Afterwards I perceived the wolves greatly prevailing over the sheep with their whole force. The sheep cried out; and their Lord came to them.
71He began to strike the wolves, who commenced a grievous lamentation; but the sheep were silent, nor from that time did they cry out.
72I then looked at them, until they departed from the wolves. The eyes of the wolves were blind, who went out and followed them with all their might. But the Lord of the sheep proceeded with them, and conducted them.
73All his sheep followed him.
74His countenance was terrific and splendid, and glorious was his aspect. Yet the wolves began to follow the sheep, until they overtook them in a certain lake of water.
75Then that lake became divided; the water standing up on both sides before their face.
76And while their Lord was conducting them, he placed himself between them and the wolves.
77The wolves however perceived not the sheep, but went into the midst of the lake, following them, and running after them into the lake of water.
78But when they saw the Lord of the sheep, they turned to fly from before his face.
79Then the water of the lake returned, and that suddenly, according to its nature. It became full, and was raised up, until it covered the wolves. And I saw that all of them which had followed the sheep perished, and were drowned.
80But the sheep passed over this water, proceeding to a wilderness, which was without both water and grass. And they began to open their eyes and to see.
81Then I beheld the Lord of the sheep inspecting them, and giving them water and grass.
82The sheep already mentioned was proceeding with them, and conducting them.
83And when he had ascended the top of the lofty rock, the Lord of the sheep sent him to them.
84Afterwards I perceived their Lord standing before them, with an aspect terrific and severe.
85And when they all beheld him, they were frightened at his countenance.
86All of them were alarmed, and trembled. They cried out after that sheep; and to the other sheep who had been with him, and who was in the midst of them, saying, We are not able to stand before our Lord, or to look upon him.
87Then that sheep who conducted them went away, and ascended the top of the rock;
88When the rest of the sheep began to grow blind, and to wander from the path which he had shown them; but he knew it not.
89Their Lord however was moved with great indignation against them; and when that sheep had learned what had happened,
90He descended from the top of the rock, and coming to them, found that there were many,
91Which had become blind;
92And had wandered from his path. As soon as they beheld him, they feared, and trembled at his presence;
93And became desirous of returning to their fold,
94Then that sheep, taking with him other sheep, went to those which had wandered.
95And afterwards began to kill them. They were terrified at his countenance. Then he caused those which had wandered to return; who went back to their fold.
96I likewise saw there in the vision, that this sheep became a man, built a sacred house for the Lord of the sheep, and made them all stand in the house.
97I perceived also that the sheep which proceeded to meet this sheep, their conductor, died. I saw, too, that all the great sheep perished, while smaller ones rose up in their place, entered into a pasture, and approached a river of water.
98Then that sheep, their conductor, who became a man, was separated from them, and died.
99All the sheep sought after him, and cried for him with bitter lamentation.
100I saw likewise that they ceased to cry after that sheep, and passed over the river of water.
101And that there arose other sheep, all of whom conducted them, instead of those who were dead, and who had previously conducted them.
102Then I saw that the sheep entered into a goodly place, and a territory delectable and glorious.
103I saw also that they became satiated; that their house was in the midst of a delectable territory; and that sometimes their eyes were opened, and that sometimes they were blind; until another sheep arose and conducted them. He brought them all back; and their eyes were opened.
104Then dogs, foxes, and wild boars began to devour them, until again another sheep arose, the master of the flock, one of themselves, a ram, to conduct them.
105This ram began to butt on every side those dogs, foxes, and wild boars, until they all perished. His eyes, and saw the ram in the midst of them, who had laid aside his glory.
106And he began to strike the sheep, treading upon them, and behaving himself without dignity.
107Then their Lord sent the former sheep again to a still different sheep, and raised him up to be a ram, and to conduct them instead of that sheep who had laid aside his glory.
108Going therefore to him, and conversing with him alone, he raised up that ram, and made him a prince and leader of the flock. All the time that the dogs troubled the sheep,
109The first ram paid respect to this latter ram.
110Then the latter ram arose, and fled away from before his face. And I saw that those dogs caused the first ram to fall.
111But the latter ram arose, and conducted the smaller sheep.
112That ram likewise begat many sheep, and died.
113Then there was a smaller sheep, a ram, instead of him, which became a prince and leader, conducting the flock.
114And the sheep increased in size, and multiplied.
115And all the dogs, foxes, and wild boars feared, and fled away from him.
116That ram also struck and killed all the wild beasts, so that they could not again prevail in the midst of the sheep, nor at any time ever snatch them away.
117And that house was made large and wide; a lofty watch tower being built upon it by the sheep, for the Lord of the sheep.
118The house was low, but the watch tower was elevated and very high.
119Then the Lord of the sheep stood upon that watch tower, and caused a full table to approach before him.
120Again I saw that those sheep wandered, and went various ways, forsaking that their house;
121And that their Lord called to some among them, whom he sent to them.
122But these the sheep began to kill. And when one of them was saved from slaughter he leaped, and cried out against those who were desirous of killing him.
123But the Lord of the sheep delivered him from their hands, and made him ascend to him, and remain with him.
124He sent also many others to them, to testify, and with lamentations to exclaim against them.
125Again I saw, when some of them forsook the house of their Lord, and his tower; wandering on all sides, and growing blind,
126I saw that the Lord of the sheep made a great slaughter among them in their pasture, until they cried out to him in consequence of that slaughter.
127Then he departed from the place of his habitation, and left them in the power of lions, tigers, wolves, and the zeebt, (Hyenas) and in the power of foxes, and of every beast.
128And the wild beasts began to tear them.
129I saw, too, that he forsook the house of their fathers, and their tower; giving them all into the power of lions to tear and devour them; into the power of every beast.
130Then I began to cry out with all my might, imploring the Lord of the sheep, and showing him how the sheep were devoured by all the beasts of prey.
131But he looked on in silence, rejoicing that they were devoured, swallowed up, and carried off; and leaving them in the power of every beast for food.
132He called also seventy shepherds, and resigned to them the care of the sheep, that they might overlook them;
133Saying to them and to their associates, Every one of you henceforwards overlook the sheep, and whatsoever I command you, do; and I will deliver them to you numbered.
134I will tell you which of them shall be slain; these destroy. And he delivered the sheep to them.
135Then he called to another, and said, Understand, and watch everything which the shepherds shall do to these sheep; for many more of them shall perish than I have commanded.
136Of every excess and slaughter, which the shepherds shall commit, there shall be an account; as, how many may have perished by my command, and how many they may have destroyed of their own heads.
137Of all the destruction brought about by each of the shepherds there shall be an account; and according to the number I will cause a recital to be made before me, how many they have destroyed of their own heads, and how many they have delivered up to destruction, that I may have this testimony against them; that I may know all their proceedings; and that, delivering the sheep to them, I may see what they will do; whether they will act as I have commanded them, or not.
138Of this, however, they shall be ignorant; neither shall you make any explanation to them, neither shall you reprove them; but there shall be an account of all the destruction done by them in their respective seasons. Then they began to kill, and destroy more than it was commanded them.
139And they left the sheep in the power of the lions, so that very many of them were devoured and swallowed up by lions and tigers; and wild boars preyed upon them. That tower they burnt, and overthrew that house.
140Then I grieved extremely on account of the tower, and because the house of the sheep was overthrown.
141Neither was I afterwards able to perceive whether they again entered that house.
142The shepherds likewise, and their associates, delivered them to all the wild beasts, that they might devour them.
143Each of them in his season, according to his number, was delivered up; each of them, one with another, was described in a book, how many of them, one with another, were destroyed, in a book.
144Then I began to weep, and was greatly indignant, on account of the sheep.
145In like manner also I saw in the vision him who wrote, how he wrote down one, destroyed by the shepherds, every day.
146He ascended, remained, and exhibited each of his books to the Lord of the sheep, containing all which they had done, and all which each of them had made away with;
147And all which they had delivered up to destruction.
148He took the book up in his hands, read it, sealed it, and deposited it.
149After this, I saw shepherds overlooking for twelve hours.
150And behold three of the sheep departed, arrived, went in; and began building all which was fallen down of that house.
151But the wild boars hindered them, although they prevailed not.
152Again they began to build as before, and raised up that tower, which was called a lofty tower.
153And again they began to place before the tower a table, with every impure and unclean kind of bread upon it.
154Moreover also all the sheep were blind, and could not see, as were the shepherds likewise.
155Thus were they delivered up to the shepherds for a great destruction, who trod them under foot, and devoured them.
156Yet was their Lord silent, until all the sheep in the field were destroyed. The shepherds and the sheep were all mixed together; but they did not save them from the power of the beasts.
157Then he who wrote the book ascended, exhibited it, and read it at the residence of the Lord of the sheep.
158He petitioned him for them, and prayed, pointing out every act of the shepherds, and testifying before him against them all. Then taking the book, he deposited it with him, and departed.
