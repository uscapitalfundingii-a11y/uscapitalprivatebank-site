Article 121 - (1578) Protestant Church (of Scotland)

Thus, from its very founding documents, the Church of Scotland has been dedicated to enslaving the minds of men in ignorance to the false teachings and doctrines such as Original Sin in honor of Satan as Sephiroth; and
