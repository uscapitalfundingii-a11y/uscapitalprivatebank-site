I.   Introductory provisions

1.2 Concepts

Article 9 - Medicine

Canon 4673 (link)

Medicine is an ancient term meaning the occult art of remedy and cure. The word "Medicine" is now more commonly recognized as both a “science” and a commercial global cartel of private guilds and pharmaceutical interests controlling almost all industries of therapy and health.

Canon 4674 (link)

The word "Medicine" originates from the Latin medicina meaning “cure, charm, relief”. It originates from the root Latin word mede/medeis meaning “magic, spell, witchcraft”.

Canon 4675 (link)

Medicine represents the first of two (2) competing views and models to health, remedy and cure throughout most civilizations being Occult Ritual and Spells and Therapeutic Treatment:

(i) Occult Ritual and Spells is the ancient belief that illness is directly caused by malevolent forces and spiritual disharmony which can only be cured by secret ceremonies and spells; and

(ii) Therapeutic Treatment is the ancient belief that illness is both a physical and mental condition that can be cured with the correct mind and therapeutic treatment.

Canon 4676 (link)

As Medicine throughout its history is the occult art of issuing spells and rituals, the focus of Medicine has historically always been on concocting treatments based on the symptoms, not the underlying malfunction.

Canon 4677 (link)

Given Medicine requires a certain education and indoctrination of belief to comprehend certain occult ideas, historically Medicine has been confined to members of cults and the better educated upper classes of civilization.

Canon 4678 (link)

The primary threat to organized Medicine throughout history has always been genuine therapeutic treatments and the associated free availability of herbs and narcotics prescribed through therapy with curative and pain killing properties.

Canon 4679 (link)

The absurd and often lethal cures prescribed by Medicine as an occult art in treating symptoms have stretched the very limit of ethical boundaries including but not limited to consuming human flesh, blood, bloodletting, eating all manner of contagious and deadly concoctions. Despite such absurdity, Medicine is still permitted to “legally” prescribe a wide variety of deadly and totally unnecessary procedures.

Canon 4680 (link)

Organized social wide Medicine was first introduced during the 19th Century corresponding with the restructuring of society, and the demonstrations of huge profits gained from restricting local herbs and narcotics to promote the international drugs trade. It is founded on seven objectives that exist today:

(i) Legally Protected Occult Cures was and still is the first objective of organized Medicine enabling occult practicioners to control what is regarded as cures, to indemnify themselves from frequent deaths and eliminate genuine therapeutic remedy; and

(ii) Legally Protected Occult Guilds of Practitioners was and still is the second objective whereby the only people permitted to offers cures are members of private guilds, sworn to protect monopolies and the objectives of the guilds; and

(iii) Eliminate Therauptics was and still is the third objective whereby all credible and genuine therapeutic practitioners must be rooted out, discredited and eliminated; and

(iv) Eliminate Free Herbs and Narcotic Drugs was and still is the fourth objective whereby the ancient right of free herbs and narcotic drugs since the beginning of civilization must be eliminated so therapeutics have no products to use and the mass populations must purchase only “approved” medicines; and

(v) Control all Herbs and Pharmaceuticals was and still is the fifth objective whereby all herbs and pharmaceuticals were to be controlled by a handful of families and elite; and

(vi) Promote Pandemics towards Medicine was and still is the sixth objective whereby local and global pandemics are promoted to encourage the increased sale of cures, to control the population and sometimes to try and reduce the population; and

(vii) Promote Chronic Health towards Occult Medical Practitioners was and still is the seventh objective whereby chronic health is promoted by ensuring marginal or damaging health issues remain present in the lives of the population, even if one (1) carcinogenic is forced to be replaced for another.

Canon 4681 (link)

Despite the substantial body of clinical research and documentation collected to appear to convert Medicine from a highly discredited occult art into a science, Medicine by its very foundations remains a force for ill, working against the common interests of each society.

Canon 4682 (link)

Considering all the collection of clinical research and studies into the effects of natural herbs and natural narcotic drugs has been fatally compromised by the founding objectives of Medicine to vilify therapeutic treatments and to defy all of civilized history and prohibit and control the supply of free herbs and narcotic drugs, no clinical data or alleged scientific data may be used to support the continued prohibition of naturally grown herbs or narcotic drugs unless issued by a completely independent body of therapeutic treatment practitioners.

Canon 4683 (link)

Given the prohibition of naturally grown herbs and narcotic drugs is in deliberate and direct contradiction to the customs and laws of all civilized history until the advent of the cartel of Medicine and pharmaceutical companies, all laws prohibiting the natural growing of herbs and narcotic drugs for personal use are hereby null and void, having no legal effect and prohibited to be revived.

Canon 4684 (link)

As Medicine by its very definition and structure throughout societies represents a parasitoid and force for ill, it is forbidden, reprobate and not permitted to be revived. Instead, the word Therapeutic and all derivatives thereof is the proper term for genuine health, cure and treatment.

Canon 4685 (link)

As Medical Guilds have deliberately conspired to reduce valid therapies, and control the knowledge and practice of genuine therapeutic treatment, all private medical guilds registered under Roman Law are hereby reprobate, suppressed and not permitted to be revived.
