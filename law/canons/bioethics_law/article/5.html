I.   Introductory provisions

1.2 Concepts

Article 5 - Biology

Canon 4646 (link)

Biology is the term used to define a separate system of orthodoxy and dogma concerning the study of life and living organisms formed at the beginning of the 19th Century by distinguishing “living things” from “non living things”.

Canon 4647 (link)

The word Biology was first created in 1802 by German G. Reinhold Treviranus and Frenchman Jean-Baptiste Lamarck by combining the ancient Greek words bios meaning "life" and logia meaning "study of". The creation of the concept of Biology then separated and isolated the concepts of life and “living” to select structures from the broader study of physical nature (ancient Greek word φύσις (phusis)).

Canon 4648 (link)

From its very inception, Biology was based on proving and protecting three (3) key foundation doctrines being material reality of existence, enclosure of life from non-life and the isolation and enclosure of beliefs of spirit/essence and soul:

(i) The first founding doctrine of Biology is that existence is defined by material reality of things, made up of common building blocks of matter, without the necessity nor evidence of supernatural forces; and

(ii) The second founding doctrine of Biology is that living things are distinct from non living things and that non-living things do not share the same qualities as living things; and

(iii) The third founding doctrine of Biology is that there is no physical evidence of the material existence of the soul, nor of any particular essence in living things.

Canon 4649 (link)

The success of Biology in proving and self referencing itself to support its founding doctrines has led to the modern framework of nine (9) orthodoxy and dogma of biology being Soul, Will, Cells, Genes, Chemical, Homeostatis, Energy, Evolution and Self-Selection:

(i) The Biology doctrine of Soul is the absolute “scientific” claim in the Non-existence of soul, being the absence of any essence, spirit exhibited in all organisms; and

(ii) The Biology doctrine of Will is the absolute “scientific” claim is Non-existence of intent (will)

(iii) The Biology doctrine of Cells is the identification of the Cell as the basic building block and unit of all Life; and

(iv) The Biology doctrine of Genes is the almost total general belief that Genes are the sole unit of inheritance within DNA and chromosomes and that the vast majority of DNA (once called “junk DNA” in biology) plays little or no influence in determining life; and

(v) The Biology doctrine of Chemical is the belief that all change within an organism may be viewed and isolated for its chemical properties so that such chemical properties may be influenced or artificially “regulated” through pharmaceutical interventions; and

(vi) The Biology doctrine of Homeostasis is the belief that life may be defined by the process of an organism regulating chemical reactions within its internal environment to achieve homeostasis (balance); and

(vii) The Biology doctrine of Energy is the belief that energy is the unit of change and that similar to the orthodoxy of the laws of Thermodynamics, organisms convert different forms of energy into other forms of energy; and

(viii) The Biology doctrine of Evolution is the absolute belief that new species and inherited traits are the product of Evolution and not intervention, despite the overwhelming examples of genetic tampering in the present world and examples of gene splicing within the genetic code of the Homo Sapien species; and

(ix) The Biology doctrine of Self-Selection is the absolute belief that excluding a dominant species intervention, the survival of species is based on “survival of the fittest” and “evolutionary optimization”. Species that develop weak mutations for the environment “self select” for extinction while strong survive, despite the fact that the Homo Sapien species possess no less than ten (10) mutations that should have caused extinction of the species, if the species had evolved without civilization like all other life on Earth.

Canon 4650 (link)

Individual discoveries and knowledge attributed to Biology as a whole may be re-classified under different structures, devoid of deliberate orthodoxy and dogma, without compromising the value of the scientific information of a particular discovery.

Canon 4651 (link)

Considering Biology is crippled with contradictions against observable natural phenomena and given it is a discipline deliberately designed to reinforce certain philosophical arguments through the manipulation of scientific data, biology cannot possibly be considered a credible science.

Canon 4652 (link)

As Biology is deliberately and fatally flawed, the term and science of BIOTICS consistent with Divine Law and Natural Law is used.
