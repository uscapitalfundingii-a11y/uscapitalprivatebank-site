II.   Life

2.2 Genetics

Article 30 - HydroCarbon Genome

Canon 4784 (link)

The most stable chemical system for transmitting the knowledge of cellular and protein construction are the RNA and DNA molecules (genetic code). All cells possess either RNA, DNA or both. The arrangement of molecular bases within RNA and DNA strands are in turn decoded within the universal functions of cells as instructions for the production and maintenance of cellular activity.

Canon 4785 (link)

Some genetic code stores specific instructions that are critical to the growth and function of a species. These particular strands of DNA or RNA are called “genes” or “gene sequences”. The principle difference between complex species can be found in the unique variations of genes within their DNA Genome.

Canon 4786 (link)

Men and women are forbidden to claim ownership as property any physical element of animal life as such a false claim directly contradicts the obligations of their office as stewards of all life on Earth.

Canon 4787 (link)

Corporations, persons, trusts, associations, estates or any other fictional entity are forbidden to claim ownership as property any physical element of animal life including but not limited to genetic material.

Canon 4788 (link)

Any law that falsely permits claims of ownership of genetic material is automatically rendered unnatural and inferior law by such gross error in permitting patently false law to be rendered in the first instance.
