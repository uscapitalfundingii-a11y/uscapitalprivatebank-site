VI.   Homo Sapien Life

6.6 Homo Sapien Elder

Article 129 - Homo Sapien Elder

Canon 5349 (link)

Homo Sapien Elder is defined as the period from the age of the seventieth (70th) birthday until death.

Canon 5350 (link)

Every Elder is entitled to the safety and assistance of adequate shelter and residence.

Canon 5351 (link)

Every Elder is entitled to the safety and assistance of adequate shelter and residence.No person is permitted to be denied a place to sleep and the safety and support of food and liquid.

Canon 5352 (link)

It is an essential principle that all Elders have a right to a quality and dignified life. Laws or belief systems that enforce life without any consideration of the quality of life shall always be considered barbaric, cruel and against the very meaning of life they claim to cherish.
