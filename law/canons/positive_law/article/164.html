V.   Occurrence

5.2 Fact

Article 164 - Methodology

Canon 2488 (link)

Method of Fact is the approach by which a Fact may be discovered, prepared, obtained through the proper measurement in the pursuit of knowledge and investigation.

Canon 2489 (link)

The word Methodology originates from the Ancient Greek word μέθοδος (methodos) meaning “pursuit of knowledge, investigation and the mode and system of prosecuting such inquiry”.

Canon 2490 (link)

A Methodology for the pursuit and discovery of facts is valid only when its elements are in accordance with these Canons.

Canon 2491 (link)

A claimed Fact obtained through a methodology not consistent with these Canons is itself invalid.
