II. Divine Systems
 
  2.4 Divine System of Spirit  
  Article 34-WE  
  Canon 268  
  WE is the collective spirit of all HOMO SAPIEN spirits with whom an individual of the species currently interacts, or has ever interacted at any time during their existence.  
  Canon 269  
  The spirit of WE is part of the greater collective spirit of AEON, which is part of the greater collective spirit of ANIMUS, which is part of the greater collective spirit of CORPUS, which is part of the greater collective spirit of GAIA, which is part of the greater collective spirit of SOL, which is part of the greater collective spirit of GAL, which is part of the greater collective spirit of UNITAS, which is part of the greater collective spirit of ALL.
