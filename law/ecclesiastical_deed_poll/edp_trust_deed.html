Deed of Trust is a condensed version of the sacred covenant Pactum de Singularis Caelum suitable for use in the registration of accounts and other services through alternate societies where the lodgment of a circumscribed copy of the covenant is inappropriate.  
     
  Under Article 130.2 of the sacred Covenant Pactum de Singularis Caelum, members may issue a Deed of Trust as proof of the existence of their Trust when seeking a service or registration with an alternate society.  
  Whie the complete covenant is the formal deed by which all trusts of members is lawfully created and property conveyed, it is often impractical to submit a bulky copy of the whole covenant when seeking the registration of a special deposit trust account for example. The Deed of Trust therefore makes this process possible, conforming in respect to standards and traditions of alternate societies.
